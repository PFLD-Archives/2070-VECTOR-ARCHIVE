# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION: RHODES SPACE STATION (RSS)
## Project: Mobile Logistic & Repair Foundry
## Operator: United Columbus (U.C.) Military / Parafield Security Operations (P.S.O.)
## Finalized Date: October 2074

### I. OVERVIEW
The Rhodes Space Station (RSS) is the logistical backbone of Columbian's network. As a "Pawn" class station, its primary function is to sustain the fleet during long-term liberation campaigns. The RSS is designed to FTL-jump into "Cold Zones" (secure areas behind the front lines) to provide immediate repair, refueling, and resupply for P.S.O. and U.C. vessels. Began construction in 2069 and entered operational status in 2074.

### II. CORE SPECIFICATIONS
* **Dimensions:** 10km (Length) x 6km (Width - Wing Expansion)
* **Mass:** 200 Million Metric Tons (Variable based on cargo load)
* **Estimated Cost:** $145.0 Trillion (2074 Credits)
* **Crew Capacity:** 45,000 Total
    * 5,000 Military Security
    * 35,000 Engineering and Industrial Personnel
    * 5,000 P.S.O. Maintenance Synthetics
* **Primary Power Plant:** S.F.R. Mark II (Low-Yield / Type-C)
    * **Description:** The Type-C reactor is optimized for industrial output. It provides the immense electrical load required for large-scale molecular 3D printing, magnetic docking tethers, and mass-driver fuel transfers.

### III. TACTICAL & INDUSTRIAL SYSTEMS
The RSS is not a frontline combatant, but it possesses specialized systems that provide a different kind of "advantage" in a sector.

#### 1. Integrated Dry-Dock (The Cradle)
The central spine of the RSS features a massive open-frame hangar capable of housing P.S.O vessels for deployment.
* **Mechanism:** Uses S.F.R-powered tractor beams and thousands of automated repair drones.
* **Tactical Application:** Can perform emergency hull sealing and reactor stabilization in a fraction of the time required by a stationary shipyard.

#### 2. Molecular Forge Arrays
A series of high-capacity industrial fabricators located in the station’s wings.
* **Mechanism:** Converts raw harvested materials into munitions, spare parts, and even synthetic chassis using the Mark II’s thermal energy.
* **Tactical Application:** Allows the RSS to act as an infinite magazine for the fleet, manufacturing railgun slugs and missiles on-site during a siege.

#### 3. Kinetic Suppression Shielding
Since the RSS is a high-value target with less offensive weaponry, it utilizes a "Hard-Light" defensive grid.
* **Mechanism:** Projects a physical barrier of solid photons to deflect long-range orbital bombardment.
* **Tactical Application:** Protects both the RSS and any ships currently docked within its "Cradle" from opportunistic enemy strikes.

### IV. OPERATIONAL DEPLOYMENT: THE RE-ARM STRATEGY
The RSS rarely engages the enemy directly. It's FTL drive is calibrated for precision jumps into debris fields where it can harvest scrap and turn it back into usable resources for the U.C. Military.

### V. CAVEATS DATA
* The RSS is slow-moving in sub-light speeds due to its massive industrial footprint.
* Overloading the Molecular Forge Arrays can cause reactor spikes, requiring a temporary shutdown of all non-essential life support.
