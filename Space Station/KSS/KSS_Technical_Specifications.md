# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION: KRIEGER SPACE STATION (KSS)
## Project: Siege-Class Orbital Artillery Platform
## Operator: United Columbus (U.C.) Military / Parafield Security Operations (P.S.O.)
## Date: November 2072

### I. OVERVIEW
The Krieger Space Station (KSS) was developed in late 2072 to address the need for extreme-range suppression. As the most aggressive "Pawn" in the Parafield arsenal, the KSS acts as a sniper on a galactic scale. It is often deployed to the edge of a solar system to bombard enemy installations with impunity, using its superior optics and S.F.R.-powered rail systems.

### II. CORE SPECIFICATIONS
* **Dimensions:** 7km (Length) x 2.5km (Diameter)
* **Mass:** 95 Million Metric Tons
* **Development Year:** 2072
* **Estimated Cost:** $85 Trillion (Adjusted 2072 Credits)
* **Crew Capacity:** 6,000 Total
    * 2,000 Technical Gunnery Officers
    * 3,000 P.S.O. Security Synthetics
    * 1,000 Strategic Analysts
* **Primary Power Plant:** S.F.R. Mark II (Low-Yield / Type-K)
    * **Description:** The Type-K reactor is a high-discharge variant. It is designed to store massive amounts of energy in capacitor banks, allowing for "Alpha-Strike" bursts that power the station's primary rail-artillery.

### III. TACTICAL ARMAMENTS
The KSS is built around its primary weapon system, with the rest of the station serving as a housing for the cooling and targeting arrays.

#### 1. The "Gungnir" Rail-Artillery Array
A massive, longitudinal railgun that runs the entire 7km length of the station.
* **Mechanism:** Uses the S.F.R. Type-K to create a series of sequential electromagnetic gates, accelerating a 50-ton tungsten-carbide slug to relativistic speeds.
* **Tactical Application:** Precision bombardment of planetary targets or orbital shipyards from outside the target's effective return-fire range.

#### 2. Vector-Thrust Interceptor Bays
Because the KSS is vulnerable at close range, it houses specialized interceptor drones.
* **Mechanism:** Automated P.S.O. drones equipped with short-range lasers.
* **Tactical Application:** Screens the station from incoming torpedoes or small-craft boarding parties while the Gungnir array recharges.

#### 3. Deep-Space Thermal Masking
Unlike the MSS or JSS, the KSS utilizes stealth-based defense.
* **Mechanism:** Vents the S.F.R.’s heat into internal heat sinks rather than external radiators for short periods.
* **Tactical Application:** Allows the KSS to remain "cold" and invisible to long-range thermal sensors while positioning for a siege.

### IV. OPERATIONAL DEPLOYMENT: THE "LONG-REACH" STRATEGY
The KSS is usually the first to arrive at a conflict and the last to leave. Its FTL drive is calibrated for "Long-Jump" accuracy, allowing it to drop out of warp behind moons or within asteroid belts to hide its signature while preparing a bombardment.

### V. CAVEATS DATA
* Firing the Gungnir array creates a massive "Thermal Bloom" that reveals the station's location to all sensors in the sector.
* The station possesses minimal hull armor to accommodate the massive railgun capacitors; if caught in a CQC dogfight, it relies entirely on its interceptor drones.
