# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION: CHEN SPACE STATION (CSS)
## Project: Rapid Response / Expeditionary Deployment Platform
## Operator: United Columbus (U.C.) Military / Parafield Security Operations (P.S.O.)
## Finalized Date: May 2073

### I. OVERVIEW
The Chen Space Station (CSS) was developed in 2073 to streamline the "Liberation Protocol" for the Columbian Empire. It serves as a mobile barracks and heavy-drop facility. Unlike traditional troop carriers, the CSS is a hardened orbital platform capable of surviving intense planetary defense fire while deploying an entire army's worth of reinforcements and heavy Titans within minutes of FTL arrival. Began construction in 2068 and entered operational status in 2073.

### II. CORE SPECIFICATIONS
* **Dimensions:** 9.5km (Length) x 4.5km (Diameter)
* **Mass:** 165 Million Metric Tons
* **Finalized Year:** 2073
* **Estimated Cost:** $115 Trillion (2073 Credits)
* **Crew Capacity:** 65,000 Total
    * 5,000 Station Operations Staff
    * 10,000 U.C. Expeditionary Marines
    * 50,000 P.S.O. Synthetic Combatants
* **Storage Capacity:** 250 Aegis-Class Titans / 5,000 Drop-Pods
* **Primary Power Plant:** S.F.R. Mark II (Low-Yield / Type-D)
    * **Description:** The Type-D (Deployment) reactor is built for high-frequency gravitational manipulation. It powers the massive tractor beams and launch rails required to safely (and rapidly) propel heavy mechanical units into a planet's atmosphere.

### III. TACTICAL & DEPLOYMENT SYSTEMS
The CSS is designed to transition from FTL exit to full-scale invasion in under 180 seconds.

#### 1. The "Titan-Fall" Magnetic Catapult
A series of twelve high-velocity launch tubes running vertically through the station's core.
* **Mechanism:** Uses S.F.R. magnetic acceleration to fire Titan-class mechs and heavy armor directly into a planetary atmosphere.
* **Tactical Application:** Ensures reinforcements reach the ground at speeds that bypass most conventional surface-to-air interceptors.

#### 2. Orbital Suppression "Rain"
A defensive-offensive hybrid system located on the station's ventral (downward-facing) hull.
* **Mechanism:** Small-bore fusion repeaters and kinetic dispensers.
* **Tactical Application:** Provides "Danger Close" fire support for the troops it just deployed, clearing landing zones of enemy infantry and light vehicles.

#### 3. Gravity-Link Tether
A specialized gravitational beam used to retrieve units or stabilize falling debris.
* **Mechanism:** Focuses S.F.R. energy into a concentrated "tractor" stream.
* **Tactical Application:** Allows the CSS to "pluck" damaged Titans or critical personnel off the battlefield and return them to the station for immediate repair by the onboard engineering teams.

### IV. OPERATIONAL DEPLOYMENT: THE "IRON RAIN" STRATEGY
The CSS strategy relies on overwhelming the enemy's processing power. By dropping thousands of P.S.O. synthetics and dozens of Titans simultaneously, the enemy's defensive grid is forced to choose between targeting the station or the thousands of incoming threats falling from the sky.

### V. CAVEATS DATA
* Atmospheric drag during mass deployment creates significant heat; the CSS must maintain a specific orbital pitch to avoid "cooking" its own deployment bays.
* The station is highly dependent on the MSS for escort during the initial 300 seconds of a deployment when its shields are redirected to power the Titan Catapults.
