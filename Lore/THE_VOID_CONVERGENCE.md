# LORE: THE VOID CONVERGENCE

## I. THE KRIEGER ANOMALY (2088)
While the Great Galactic War between the Columbian Empire (TCE) and The Soviet Hegemony (TSH) consumed the majority of Parafield's tactical focus, the **Krieger Space Station (KSS)** was performing long-range deep-space scans on the fringes of the Perseus Arm. 

In November 2088, the KSS’s "Gungnir" targeting array—recalibrated for passive multi-spectral surveillance—intercepted a series of non-repeating, high-frequency signals originating from the "Boötes Void" sector.

## II. DATA DECRYPTION: THE "HORROR" PROTOCOL
Initial analysis by Parafield's Vector-class AIs labeled the data as background cosmic noise. However, a specialized analytical sub-routine on the KSS, overseen by Strategic Analysts, bypassed the standard filters.

The decrypted data revealed:
*   **Biological Signatures:** Traces of hyper-accelerated evolution that did not follow carbon-based or silicon-based paths known to humanity.
*   **Systemic Consumption:** Visual data confirmed the "death" of multiple star systems—not through supernova or natural decay, but through a process of "molecular stripping."
*   **The Fleet:** Distorted captures of massive, organic-mechanical constructs that dwarfed even the Jenson Space Station (JSS).

## III. PARAFIELD'S CLASSIFIED DISCOVERY
Parafield Corporation (PFLD) realized that humanity was not alone, but more importantly, that the "others" were predatory. These entities, colloquially dubbed **"The Void Convergence,"** appeared to be a galactic-scale apex predator moving toward the Sol Galaxy.

The horrors confirmed that while TCE and TSH were fighting for territory, something was coming to consume the very matter they were fighting over.

## IV. STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS
The KSS remains the primary sentinel for this threat. Parafield has kept this information restricted to High Command and the Parafield Executive Board, fearing that public knowledge would cause a total collapse of the war effort against the Soviets. 

The "Void" data suggests that the Convergence is not interested in diplomacy, territory, or ideology. It is a hunger that cannot be reasoned with.
