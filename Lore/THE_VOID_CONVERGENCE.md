# LORE: THE VOID CONVERGENCE

## I. KRIEGER ANOMALY (2088)
While the Great Galactic War between the Columbian Empire (TCE) and The Soviet Hegemony (TSH) consumed the majority of Parafield's tactical focus, the Krieger Space Station (KSS) was performing long-range deep-space scans on the fringes of the Perseus Arm. 

In November 2088, the KSS Gungnir targeting array—recalibrated for passive multi-spectral surveillance—intercepted a series of non-repeating, high-frequency signals originating from the Boötes Void sector.

## II. DATA DECRYPTION: THE HORRORS
Initial analysis by Parafield Vector-class AIs labeled the data as background cosmic noise. However, a specialized analytical sub-routine on the KSS, overseen by Strategic Analysts, bypassed standard filters.

The decrypted data revealed:
*   **Biological Signatures:** Traces of hyper-accelerated evolution that did not follow carbon-based or silicon-based paths known to humanity.
*   **Systemic Consumption:** Visual data confirmed the total destruction of multiple star systems through molecular stripping.
*   **The Fleet:** Distorted captures of massive, organic-mechanical constructs that dwarfed even the Jenson Space Station (JSS).

## III. PARAFIELD CLASSIFIED DISCOVERY
Parafield Corporation (PFLD) confirmed that humanity was not alone, but more importantly, the extraterrestrial presence was predatory. These entities, identified as The Void Convergence, appear to be a galactic-scale apex predator moving toward the Sol Galaxy.

The data confirms that while TCE and TSH fight for territory, an external force is approaching to consume the very matter they contest.

## IV. STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS
The KSS remains the primary sentinel for this threat. Parafield has restricted this information to High Command and the Parafield Executive Board to prevent a total collapse of the war effort against the Soviets. 

The Convergence data suggests these entities are not interested in diplomacy, territory, or ideology. They represent a biological hunger that cannot be reasoned with.
