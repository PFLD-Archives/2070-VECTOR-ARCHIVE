# FRACTION: THE CONVERGENCE
**Status:** Extraterrestrial Apex Predator / Existential Threat
**Origin:** Boötes Void (Deep Space)
**Classification:** Organic-Mechanical Gestalt

---

## I. OVERVIEW
The Convergence is not a civilization in the human sense. They are a biological and mechanical hunger that has traversed the void between galaxies. Their arrival in the Sol Galaxy was first detected by the Krieger Space Station (KSS) through the interception of the Horror Protocol data. They do not communicate, negotiate, or occupy territory; they consume it, stripping entire star systems of their molecular matter to fuel their relentless expansion.

---

## II. BIOLOGICAL & MECHANICAL COMPOSITION
Convergence entities are a seamless blend of hyper-evolved organic tissue and advanced crystalline-metallic structures. They do not utilize traditional propulsion or power plants, instead drawing energy directly from the molecular breakdown of the matter they consume.

---

## III. KNOWN CONVERGENCE ENTITIES

### 1. THE HARVESTER (DREADNOUGHT CLASS)
The primary consumption vessel of the Convergence. A massive, shifting mass of organic-metallic tendrils and crystalline maws.
*   **Role:** Molecular Stripping / System Consumption.
*   **Mechanism:** Deploys a concentrated graviton field that breaks down planets and moons into their base elements, which are then absorbed into the Harvester's core.
*   **Threat Level:** Extreme. Dwarfs the Jenson Space Station (JSS) in sheer mass.

### 2. THE VOID-STALKER (INFILTRATOR CLASS)
A sleek, bio-mechanical predator designed for deep-space interception and scouting.
*   **Role:** Reconnaissance / Asset Neutralization.
*   **Mechanism:** Utilizes organic-based cloaking that renders it invisible to most conventional TCE and TSH sensors. Equipped with crystalline-tipped appendages that can slice through heavy ablative shielding.
*   **Threat Level:** High. Known for "hunting" isolated patrol ships and research outposts.

### 3. THE SWARM-DRONE (ASSAULT CLASS)
Smaller, highly mobile units that travel in massive, coordinated clouds.
*   **Role:** Area Denial / Swarm Attack.
*   **Mechanism:** They lack individual intelligence, instead operating as a single hive-mind. They use high-velocity kinetic impacts and short-range plasma discharges to overwhelm enemy defenses.
*   **Threat Level:** Moderate individually, Lethal in swarms.

### 4. THE NEURAL-BLIGHTER (EW CLASS)
A specialized entity designed to disrupt and "corrupt" digital and neural systems.
*   **Role:** Electronic Warfare / Psychological Disruption.
*   **Mechanism:** Emits a high-frequency "scream" that targets the neural links of Vector-class AI and the communications of TSH units. 
*   **Effect:** Causes immediate neural feedback for pilots and can temporarily "lobotomize" local AI units.

---

## IV. STRATEGIC ANALYSIS
Traditional military doctrine is ineffective against the Convergence. They do not possess a centralized command structure or vulnerable logistics chain. To fight them is to fight a storm. Their presence in the galaxy represents the ultimate deadline for humanity—if the Great Galactic War is not resolved, there will be nothing left to fight over.
