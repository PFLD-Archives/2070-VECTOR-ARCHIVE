# FRACTION: THE SOVIET HEGEMONY (TSH)
**Status:** Hostile Superpower / Emerging Galactic Hegemon
**Origin:** Sol System (Neo-Soviet Resurgence)
**Leader:** General Secretary of the Supreme Galactic Soviet

---

## I. OVERVIEW
The Soviet Hegemony (TSH) is the direct successor to the reformed Soviet Union of the early 2070s. After their narrow defeat by the Columbian Empire (TCE) during the "Seven-Month War," the Soviets retreated into the deep-space sectors of the Perseus Arm. In the decades that followed, they transitioned from a broken remnant into a hyper-industrialized war machine, fueled by a singular, burning directive: the reclamation of the Sol System and the total dismantling of the Columbian Empire.

Unlike the individualistic and AI-integrated philosophy of TCE, the TSH emphasizes **Collective Synchronization**. Their society is a rigid, militarized hierarchy where every citizen—human or synthetic—is a gear in the state's massive industrial engine.

---

## II. MILITARY DOCTRINE: THE "IRON CURTAIN" STRATEGY
The TSH does not rely on the "finesse" of Vector-class AI personalities. Instead, they favor overwhelming mass, brute-force kinetic energy, and hardened electronic warfare suites designed to "choke" TCE’s neural links.

### 1. The Orbital "Great Wall"
The TSH's most terrifying achievement is the construction of a **"Great Wall" of Orbital Defense Platforms** across the borders of their occupied sectors. 
*   **Mechanism:** Tens of thousands of automated, interconnected "Citadel" stations equipped with long-range graviton beams and massive rail-artillery.
*   **Strategic Intent:** It acts as a physical and digital barrier, preventing any TCE incursion while providing a staging ground for their own inevitable "Grand Offensive" toward Sol.

### 2. Mass-Produced "Steel Guard" Synthetics
While TCE treats its Vector-class mechs as partners, the TSH views their synthetic forces as expendable hardware.
*   **The Drone Swarm:** Thousands of "Steel Guard" units are deployed simultaneously, controlled by a centralized Hive-Mind (The Red-Grid). 
*   **Tactical Advantage:** They lack the "emotional" variance of TCE mechs, making them fearless and immune to many forms of psychological or neural-based disruption.

### 3. Kinetic Decelerators
A specialized technology developed specifically to counter TCE’s high-mobility Aegis units.
*   **Mechanism:** Deployable fields that increase local gravitational constant or use "Static-Clouds" to induce physical drag on thrusters.
*   **Effect:** Strips the "Vanguard" and "Sentinel" units of their speed, forcing them into the TSH’s preferred theater: a slow, grinding war of attrition.

---

## III. THE "RECLAMATION" MANDATE
TSH propaganda is centered on the **"Great Return."** They view TCE as an imperialist usurper that "stole" the cradle of humanity while the Soviets were weakened. Every TSH vessel, from the smallest interceptor to the massive "Red-October" class dreadnoughts, carries the same directive: **Break the Empire. Retake the Sun.**

## IV. KEY ASSETS
*   **The Kremlin-Class World-Ship:** A mobile capital city and fortress, currently positioned at the heart of their "Great Wall."
*   **The Vostok-Array:** A series of deep-space listening posts that have spent years mapping TCE’s secret jump-routes and station weaknesses.
*   **Zero-G Gulags:** Industrial labor camps located within asteroid belts, where harvested resources are processed 24/7 to fuel the TSH's relentless expansion.
